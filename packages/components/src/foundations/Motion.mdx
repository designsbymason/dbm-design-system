# Motion

A duration scale (120ms–600ms) and 4 easing curves. Click **Replay** on either demo below to
re-trigger the animation — numbers alone don't convey how a curve actually *feels*, especially the
difference between `standard` and `emphasized`.

## Usage guidelines

<div style={{ display: "grid", gridTemplateColumns: "1fr 1fr", gap: "var(--dbm-space-4)" }}>
<Callout tone="success" title="Do">

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` on any non-essential animation (see `Button`'s loading spinner
  for the established pattern — slowing to 4× duration rather than disabling outright, since the
  spinner's motion is also its only in-progress signal).
- Use `standard` easing by default; reach for `decelerate`/`accelerate`/`emphasized` only when the
  motion has a specific entrance/exit/premium-emphasis role to play.

</Callout>
<Callout tone="danger" title="Don't">

- Don't hardcode a transition duration or easing curve — every animated CSS property traces to
  `motion.duration.*`/`motion.easing.*`.
- Don't reach for `slow`/`slower` durations for routine hover/focus feedback — sluggish micro-
  interactions read as unresponsive, not polished.

</Callout>
</div>
