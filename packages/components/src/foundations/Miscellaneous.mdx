<Callout tone="info" title="The z-index scale is a strict layering order, not arbitrary numbers">

Each step is spaced 100 apart specifically to leave room for component-local stacking (e.g. a
dropdown's own internal layers) without ever colliding with the *next* named layer up. When adding
a new overlay-style component, place it at the step matching its actual intended stacking
relationship to existing overlays — never invent a new raw z-index number outside this scale.

</Callout>
