# ButtonGroup

**Tier:** molecule · **Category:** Inputs & Forms · **Status:** built 2026-09-26; **Finalized 2026-09-26**, declared by the user after the final review pass recorded below, with two known items left as they are (see "Finalized" at the end).

## What was built

A single component (not compound), item 18 in the itemized molecule build order (`04-component-inventory.md`): a named `role="group"` around related `Button`s and `IconButton`s, laid out as one segmented control or spaced apart. No new dependency, no new token, no component token.

Props: `variant`, `size`, `rounded` (defaults for every button inside; a button's own prop wins), `disabled` (disables every button; a button disabled itself stays disabled), `attached` (default `true`: corners squared where buttons meet, one separator, no gap; `false`: spaced by `space.2`, and a horizontal row wraps), `orientation` (a row or a column, or a mobile-first breakpoint map through `useResolvedResponsiveValue`, as `Tabs` does), `fullWidth` (a row shares the width equally; a column's buttons match the group's width), and `aria-label` / `-labelledby` / `-describedby`, `id`, `className`, `style`, `data-testid`, `children`. It is not a selection control and adds no arrow-key handling: every button is an ordinary tab stop (`Toolbar`, item 27, is the component that roves).

## Decisions taken while building

- **Settings reach the buttons through an internal context that `Button` and `IconButton` read** — the fork in the road, chosen by the user from three options, recorded as [ADR-0025](../adr/0025-buttongroup-shares-settings-through-a-context-read-by-button-and-iconbutton.md). Inside a group a button also emits `data-variant`, which the group's stylesheet reads to draw the separator that suits it. Both atoms were changed additively (see their own files).
- **Only the corners where two buttons meet are squared.** The group never sets a radius: it zeroes the inner corners with logical properties (`border-start-start-radius` and its three siblings), so a button's outer corners stay whatever they were (`radius-md`, or `radius-full` for a `rounded` one) and a right-to-left page mirrors it with no prop. The selectors are more specific than a button's own `:focus-visible` radius rule, so the meeting corners stay square while one is focused.
- **Separators, per variant:** solid fills (`primary`, `destructive`) get a one-pixel hairline in `bg.surface`, so two fills read as separated by a gap; `secondary` buttons overlap by one border width so their two borders are one shared line; `tertiary` gets a rule in `border.brand-subtle` and `ghost` one in `border.focus`, since neither has a solid fill or border to mark where one ends (both colours chosen by the user in the follow-up below). A bg token draws the solid fills' border on purpose: the surface is what shows through a gap, and no `border.*` token names that. The first build put `ghost` with the solid fills; the screenshot showed its white hairline on a pale tint nearly invisible, so it moved to a rule, first a shared faint `border.default` one.
- **Every button takes the row's height (or the column's width).** An `IconButton` is a fixed box and a `Button` with a leading icon is about 2.4px taller, so a fused row showed a step (measured in a real browser: 2.39px at `md`, 1.8px at `lg`). The group sets `height: auto` (row) or `width: auto` (column) on its children, and `IconButton` gained `min-height` / `min-width` equal to its size, so it stretches to the row but a row of only icon buttons cannot collapse. Standalone the new properties change nothing.
- **The focused button sits above its neighbours** (`position: relative; z-index: 1`), so its ring, drawn 4–6px outside the box, isn't cut off by the next button.
- **A spaced row wraps; an attached one never does** — a fused shape broken over two lines is no longer one shape. The docs say to stack a long attached row with an `orientation` map instead.
- **`disabled` is the one setting that is OR-ed, not defaulted:** a disabled group can't be undone by a button that says `disabled={false}`.

## Findings from the build

- **`Button` and `IconButton` lost their Properties-table defaults.** Moving `variant`, `size` and `rounded` off destructuring defaults (they can't tell an explicit `"primary"` from an absent prop once a group can supply one) left the Default column showing "—" on both Docs pages — found by looking at them, not by any test. Each now states its default in its stories' argTypes (`07` §4.1).
- **A control with a `mapping` hands the snippet builder its option key** — again (`07` §4.2). The Playground's `rounded` control had an "each button's own" choice, the key is a truthy string, and the first snippet read `rounded` when nothing was set. Found on the live Docs page and fixed, then the whole choice was removed (see the follow-up).
- **Real-browser assertions that were wrong, not the component:** a `secondary` group overlaps by one border width, so "the next button starts below the previous one's bottom" is off by one; an `IconButton` at `lg` differs from a fluid `Button`, which the stretch above now makes moot.

## Follow-up, 2026-09-26 (before Finalize): defaults, separator colours, controls, related cards

At explicit direction, five changes.

- **The Properties table shows defaults for `variant`, `size`, `rounded` and `disabled`:** `primary`, `md`, `false`, `false`. A group that sets nothing leaves each button its own defaults, which are those same values, so they are stated as the group's (in the stories' `table.defaultValue` and as `@default` on the types, which feed the manifest); the descriptions still say a button's own value wins.
- **Separator colours:** the rule between attached `tertiary` buttons is `border.brand-subtle`, and between `ghost` buttons `border.focus` (both were `border.default`). They are separate rules now, each with its own token row in the Docs page, and the real-browser story asserts both, resolved from their tokens, and that the two differ. Both colours fail their assertion when swapped or reverted (checked).
- **The Playground's `variant`, `size` and `rounded` controls hold real values.** The "each button's own" choice, which needed a `mapping` and was not a value the prop takes, is gone; the selects start on `primary` and `md` and `rounded` is a switch, in the native Controls panel and in the Docs page's own panel (checked: every option listed, set and reset round-trips to the canvas). The snippet writes `variant`, `size` and `rounded` only when they differ from those defaults.
- **No inert control in any story.** `rounded` had no value in the stories' args, so the native Controls panel showed "Set boolean" on every story but the Playground's; it now starts `false` at the meta level. All 14 visible stories were checked in the running Storybook: none has a "Set boolean", "Set string" or "Set object" control, and `rounded` is an interactive checkbox everywhere except the `Rounded` story, where it is pinned and shows "–".
- **The related-component cards:** the `IconButton` card shows one icon button (it showed a group of two), and the `Tooltip` card shows an open `Tooltip` beside its button (first as the bubble alone on a one-pixel anchor, then, at explicit direction, with the "Hover me" button back, and the bubble moved from above it to its left). The bubble is portaled, so the card's fixed-height preview doesn't clip it; checked in the running Storybook that it sits inside the card, about 9px from its edge.

## Final review before Finalizing, 2026-09-26

A full `06-engineering-standards.md` §9 pass, checked against the code and the running component. **Fixed in the pass (five), and four things left for a decision — two of them since fixed at explicit direction (below).**

**Fixed**

1. **A nested group dropped the outer group's settings.** An inner `ButtonGroup`'s context replaced the outer one, so an outer `variant`, `size`, `rounded` or `disabled` stopped applying to the inner buttons, and a disabled outer group left them enabled (confirmed with a probe). The inner group now keeps whatever it doesn't set from the outer one, and `disabled` is OR-ed. Four unit tests, three of which fail without the merge (checked).
2. **A hardcoded `z-index: 1`** on the focus-stacking rule, where every other layer in the system is a token. A positioned element already paints above the static siblings around it, so it is now `var(--dbm-z-index-base)`. The story asserts the position and the base layer, that the other buttons stay static, and, new, that a focused button keeps its squared meeting corners (the CSS said so, no test did); that assertion fails when the focus rule rounds them (checked).
3. **A too-wide attached row spilled out of its own box.** With `max-width: 100%` the group shrank to the container while its buttons, whose labels don't wrap, kept their width (measured: a six-button row in a 320px container hung 176px outside the group). The cap is removed, so the group is as wide as its buttons and overflows its container as one box; the docs say to stack it with an `orientation` map. A spaced row still wraps. The story asserts both, and fails with the cap put back (checked).
4. **The icon snippet and the Docs example named icons that don't exist** (`BoldIcon`, `ItalicIcon`, `UnderlineIcon`; the package has `TextBIcon`, `TextItalicIcon`, `TextUnderlineIcon`). Found only by typechecking every snippet against the real components, which had never been done for this component: all 15 (13 static, 2 generated) now typecheck, and a planted unknown prop, a bad variant and a bad orientation each fail.
5. **The 24 × 24px target size was asserted nowhere.** A hidden real-browser story measures a one-letter `Button` and an `IconButton` in a group at every size and both orientations (all pass); it fails when the children are forced small (checked).

**Left for a decision** (the second and third bullets were fixed the same day)

- **The focus ring where it crosses a neighbour's solid fill.** In an attached group of `primary` or `destructive` buttons the ring's side over the next button sits on a same-family fill: `border.focus` against `bg.brand` is 1.3–1.6:1 and against `bg.danger` 1.1–1.6:1 across the four themes, under the 3:1 non-text floor; the other three sides are on the surface at 4.2–6.5:1. `05-component-api-conventions.md` §6 already names the remedy (draw the ring in a colour that contrasts with the fill, inside the button — the way `Tabs` does in a scrolling strip). Not changed: it alters how the group looks.
- **`asChild` links in a disabled group still ran their own click handler — fixed 2026-09-26, at explicit direction.** `Button` and `IconButton` (Finalized) now guard the disabled `asChild` click in the capture phase (`onClickCapture`), so the slotted link's own handler is blocked when the button is disabled, loading or in a disabled group; both atoms stay Finalized as a defect fix. Recorded in `Button.md` and `IconButton.md`, with tests (each fails on the old guard) and a `ButtonGroup` test.
- **`Tooltip.mdx` used the same nonexistent `BoldIcon` and `ItalicIcon` — fixed 2026-09-26, at explicit direction**, and its example also wrapped them in a `<Toolbar>` that isn't built yet; it now uses `TextBIcon`, `TextItalicIcon` and a `ButtonGroup`, and typechecks. A stricter scan of every snippet and Docs page finds no other unknown icon name and no other unbuilt component. Recorded in `Tooltip.md`.
- **The `tertiary` separator is very faint** — `border.brand-subtle` is 1.08–1.24:1 against the surface, and in dark themes a `tertiary` group reads as separated words with almost no visible rule. It is the colour chosen at explicit direction and the same decorative accent used elsewhere; recorded so it is a known choice.

**Checked, no action needed**
- **Baseline:** no `any`, suppression or hex; every value in the stylesheet is a token now; JSDoc on every prop with the defaults; `{...props}` precedes `role`, the name and `data-orientation`; the no-name warning fires once and is stripped in production; SSR-safe; `StrictMode` tested; the context value is memoised.
- **Contrast, measured in all four themes:** the primary and destructive hairlines against their fills 5.1–8.5:1; the shared `secondary` border against the surface 6.1–8.5:1; the ghost rule against its fill 4.0–7.2:1.
- **Live:** dark Emerald reads correctly for every variant; hovering the middle `secondary` button fills it and leaves both shared borders intact with square corners and no shift; a keyboard-focused button's ring is drawn over its neighbour with its meeting corners square; every visible story's controls drive its canvas (attached, disabled including a linked child, size, rounded, count, orientation) with no inert control, as swept earlier.
- **Feature completeness, considered and not added:** aligning or spreading a spaced group (compose it in a `Stack`, which has `justify`); a gap size for spaced groups (the token gap is a class, so an inline `style` gap overrides it); a loading state on the group (each button already has `isLoading`, and a group-wide loading would hide which action is running); dividers between spaced buttons (they are separate on purpose); `aria-orientation` on a vertical group (`role="group"` doesn't allow it). The four gaps already named stand: `Toolbar`, `ToggleGroup`, a split button, an explicit `dir`.

**Not verified:** a real screen reader.

## Gaps named, not built

- **`Toolbar`** (item 27): roving tabindex and arrow-key movement, for a bar of many controls.
- **`ToggleGroup`** (item 19, next): the selection version, single or multiple, with a pressed state and its own keyboard model. It can reuse the context from ADR-0025.
- **A split button** (a primary action with a menu caret): a composition of a `Button` and a menu trigger inside a group, once `Menu` exists.
- **Reading the group's `dir` from a prop**: it follows the page today, like `Breadcrumb`.

## Finalized, 2026-09-26

Declared by the user after the final review: its five defects were fixed, and the two atom and docs items were fixed at explicit direction. **Two items from the review were left as they are and are known limitations of the Finalized component, not open questions:**

- **The focus ring's side over a neighbouring solid fill** (an attached group of `primary` or `destructive` buttons): 1.1–1.6:1 against `bg.brand` / `bg.danger` across the four themes, under the 3:1 non-text floor; the other three sides sit on the surface at 4.2–6.5:1, and the focused button is drawn on top so the ring is never cut off. Recorded as a second deliberate exception beside `Tabs`' `solid` variant (`05-component-api-conventions.md` §6). If it is ever addressed, the remedy `05` §6 names is a ring drawn inside the button in a colour that contrasts with the fill (`icon.on-brand` / `icon.on-danger`), for the solid variants in an attached group only.
- **The `tertiary` separator is very faint:** `border.brand-subtle` is 1.08–1.24:1 against the surface, so in dark themes a `tertiary` group reads as separated words with almost no visible rule. The colour chosen at explicit direction, the same decorative accent used elsewhere; each button is still identified by its label.

Later changes get a dated entry here, and go through `06-engineering-standards.md` §9's Finalized rules (ask first, then the three-question test).
